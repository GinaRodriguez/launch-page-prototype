Your CRM is now 
a conversation

ChatGPT makes Salesforce easier to work with. Salesforce makes ChatGPT more useful for business. OpenAI brings natural conversation and proactive AI agents. Salesforce brings the customer data, workflows, business logic, and controls that let those experiences get work done.

It’s easier than ever to get real AI value, fast

Put Dots to Work

Put Dots to Work

Work doesn't stop when a ChatGPT conversation ends. OpenAI dots are proactive agents working with your Salesforce context and trusted guardrails. They collaborate like a colleague in Slack, working toward ongoing goals. They can get pipeline updates, triage customer requests, help prepare for a meeting, or approve a change.

Ask More of Your Data with Tableau

Ask More of Your Data
with Tableau

With the Tableau plugin, people ask business questions in ChatGPT, explore trusted metrics, and get visual explanations. Leaders can investigate changing performance without hunting for the right dashboard. And users can create or update Tableau workbooks in ChatGPT and publish them back in Tableau, so insights can be used by the whole team.

Connect Marketing Performance to OpenAI Ads

Connect Marketing
Performance to
OpenAI Ads

A live Conversions API connects authorized Salesforce data to OpenAI’s ads platform. Planned Marketing Cloud and Data 360 integrations will let advertisers build audiences, manage campaigns, activate ads in ChatGPT, and attribute conversions. Commerce Cloud merchants can already sync product catalogs with ChatGPT to reach new consumers.

Extend AI Across the Enterprise with MuleSoft

Extend AI Across the
Enterprise with MuleSoft

The Salesforce in ChatGPT plugin uses existing Salesforce permissions to control what employees see and do. People review and approve changes, so work starts in ChatGPT but stays within existing controls. MuleSoft MCP Bridge turns APIs into governed MCP tools without rewriting services, letting companies securely expose workflows to AI.
